# The Hidden Role of Oil in Golf

### 

Golf combines precision, skill and strategy, but behind every round are petroleum-based products that help make the game possible. From equipment and apparel to the maintenance of golf courses, oil and its derivatives support many aspects of the sport. This video highlights the important role of petroleum-based products in the game of golf, demonstrating how oil continues to support leisure activities around the world.
